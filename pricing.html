Pricing & Subscription FAQ

Are features locked behind the paid subscription tier?

No. StudioMemory does not restrict features on the free subscription. Both subscriptions have identical features, including iCloud sync, portfolio and label printing, backups, and exports.

Does one subscription cover my Mac, my iPad, and my iPhone?

Yes. StudioMemory uses an integrated Universal License model. Your single subscription lets you use StudioMemory across your personal iPhone, iPad, and Mac simultaneously.

What happens if my paid subscription expires or is canceled?

Your database belongs entirely to you. If your paid subscription expires, you may continue to use StudioMemory to manage and export your current catalogue. You will not be able to add new works.
