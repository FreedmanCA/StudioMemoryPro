Pricing & Licensing FAQ

Are features locked behind the paid subscription tier?

No. StudioMemory Pro does not restrict features on the free license. Both licenses have identical features, including iCloud synchronization, printing, backups, and exports.

Does one license cover my Mac, my iPad, and my iPhone?

Yes. StudioMemory Pro uses an integrated Universal License model. Your single subscription purchase lets you use StudioMemory Pro across your personal iPhone, iPad, and Mac simultaneously.

What happens if my paid license expires or is canceled?

Your database belongs entirely to you. If your paid license expires, you may continue to use StudioMemory Pro to browse, search, backup, and export your current collection. You will not be able to add new works.
