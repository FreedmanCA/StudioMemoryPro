Frequently Asked Questions

General Overview

What is StudioMemory Pro?

StudioMemory Pro is a private digital archive app built specifically for artists, creators, and makers. It allows you to maintain and manage a permanent collection of your creative works natively on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Why is StudioMemory Pro different from Photos, Notes, or spreadsheets?

StudioMemory Pro integrates photos, unique work IDs, status tracking, search tools, printable PDF portfolios, QR code labels, and full archive exports into a single system designed natively for creative legacy management.

Does the app collect my personal information?

No. StudioMemory Pro collects no personal data, runs no ads, and does not require an account. Your data remains completely private, stored safely on your local devices or your private iCloud database.

Is there a Mac-only or iPad-only version?

No. StudioMemory Pro uses a single Universal License model. One purchase covers your personal iPhone, iPad, and Mac natively, allowing you to move between devices seamlessly.

Can I use StudioMemory Pro without an internet connection?

Yes. StudioMemory Pro stores data locally and works entirely offline. An internet connection is only required if you choose to use iCloud Sync or need to restore your paid license purchase.

Managing Your Collection

How do I add a new work?

  • Mac: Press Command + N or select File > New Work.
  • iPhone & iPad: Tap the + (Plus) icon at the top of the collection browser.

How do I import my existing collection or photos?

  • Photos: On iPhone and iPad, add photos directly via your camera, library, or files app. On Mac, you can select files or simply drag and drop images directly from your library, Finder, or Desktop.
  • Other Apps: Direct database import from competing archive systems is not currently supported. Works must be added using the standard photo and detail entry tools.

Can I change a work after it has been added?

Yes. Titles, photos, notes, status details, and favorite settings can be edited or reordered at any time.

How many photos can I add per work?

You can include up to 6 photos per work. The first photo in your gallery automatically serves as the primary thumbnail inside the main collection browser.

What happens when I move a work to the Trash?

The 30-Day Rule: StudioMemory Pro stores items moved to the Trash for 30 days before they are automatically and permanently deleted.

Data, Sync & Storage

Can I use the app without iCloud?

Yes. StudioMemory Pro is fully functional as a local offline database. iCloud is completely optional and is only required if you want your collection to automatically sync across your Apple devices.

Is my collection backed up automatically?

If you have iCloud Sync turned on, your collection is securely stored and backed up in your personal iCloud database. You can also manually generate Full Archive exports at any time for offline backup security.

Will my photos count against my iCloud storage?

Yes. Because your data is stored securely in your own private iCloud database, any synced works and photos count directly against your Apple iCloud storage allocation.

What happens if I get a new iPhone, iPad, or Mac?

  • With iCloud Sync: Your collection will instantly sync down to your new device as soon as you open the app, provided you are logged into the same Apple ID.
  • Without iCloud Sync: You can securely transfer your database by exporting a Full Archive on your old device and using the restore tool on your new one.

What happens if I delete the app?

⚠️ Data Warning: Deleting the app removes all locally stored data from that device. If iCloud Sync is off and you do not have a recently exported Full Archive (Zip) file, your data will be permanently lost. If sync is on, your records remain safe in iCloud.

Does StudioMemory Pro compress my photos?

StudioMemory Pro optimizes image assets internally to ensure fast app performance and low storage overhead. If you want photos taken directly inside the app to also save to your device's native system photo library in their full, original resolution, you can enable this option in Settings.

Exporting & Portability

What file formats does the export feature use?

StudioMemory Pro prioritizes data portability using industry-standard formats:

  • Full Archive: A compressed Zip file containing your raw text details (in JSON format) and full-size photos (in JPEG format).
  • Portfolios: Clean, shareable PDF documents containing photos and work details.
  • Labels: Physical roll labels featuring titles and scannable QR codes.

Can I export my collection if I stop using StudioMemory Pro?

Yes. Your data always belongs to you. You can export your full archive at any time, even if your paid license subscription has expired.

Can I print labels with QR codes?

Yes. Roll labels can be customized in the Settings menu to toggle the display of the work's title and its unique scannable QR code before printing.

Licensing & Subscriptions

What are the limits of the Free vs. Paid licenses?

  • Free License: Limited to a maximum of 15 works.
  • Paid License: Unlocks up to 2,500 works across all your personal Apple devices.

What happens if my paid license expires or is canceled?

Your existing archive remains fully accessible. If your license expires, you can continue to use StudioMemory Pro to view, search, manage, and export your current archive. The only restriction is that you will be locked from adding new works until the license is renewed.

Does StudioMemory Pro support Apple Family Sharing?

Apple Family Sharing support is not available at this time.

Technical Support & Troubleshooting

How do I contact technical support?

You can contact support by email at support@studiomemorypro.com.

How can I help technical support diagnose an issue?

If you run into an uncommon technical issue, you can generate an encrypted log file by navigating to Settings > Diagnostic Reports and sharing it with the support team.

Why won't the camera open when trying to take a photo?

This is typically caused by restricted system permissions. You can fix this by navigating to your Apple device's native system settings panel under Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and switching the toggle on for StudioMemory Pro.
